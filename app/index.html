Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, no difficulty setting, no mission replay. PowerPyx puts the run at about 200 hours; Push Square says 250 to 300. The story is 60 to 80 of those hours. Do the stages in order.

Save advice

The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, and no mission replay. PowerPyx suggests keeping a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotating the rest during free-roam cleanup. GTABase agrees: save before every chapter and before the final mission.

What looks missable but is not

Many optional missions vanish after a while, some quest givers go quiet for part of the story (Kenton does), some fences leave for good, and a few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that close. PowerPyx says none of that touches any trophy or the 100% checklist: there are far more optional missions than the 100 needed, every trophy quest giver comes back, and the magazines also spawn elsewhere.

Two warnings that are not about trophies

Cheats switch trophies off and stop the game saving (PowerPyx and Push Square agree), so keep them off your platinum save. And the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent and makes police far more aggressive: you can still earn everything, but the cleanup gets harder.
