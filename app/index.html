    The road to Vice Got a Price!, in the order to play it. Built from the official trophy descriptions and the PowerPyx roadmap: about 200 hours in total, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter; the game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave.

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam, and the 100% checklist has nothing missable on it. The game has no mission replay, so the habit that protects you is a manual save at the start of each chapter.

    Not missable, but worth knowing

    • Story trophies (Chapters 1 to 5 and the named missions) pop as you play and cannot be missed.
    • Side questlines for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone stay available after the story, even when a character goes quiet for a chapter.
    • Stranger mission strands are still in the open world after the credits.
    • Optional missions with an hourglass icon can expire, but they do not feed any trophy.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving on your save file, so keep them off a trophy run.

    Missable report: PowerPyx and GTABase, checked 19 November 2026. The Chapter 1 meditation is unconfirmed as a strict requirement; do it anyway.
