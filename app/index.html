    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no online play, no difficulty setting, no mission replay — so play in this order and keep a save at the start and end of every chapter (the game gives 15 manual save slots). PowerPyx puts the run at about 200 hours at difficulty 4/10; Push Square says up to 300 hours or more. The story takes 60–80 hours, the 100% checklist about 100, and the leftover stranger strands and odd jobs about 10.

    Missables

    Good news first: there is no mission replay and no chapter select, yet almost nothing can be missed, because free-roam stays open after the story and every quest giver tied to a trophy comes back. PowerPyx, Push Square, GTABase and KeenGamer agree on the same single exception.

    Save habits that cover the rest

    • Keep a manual save at the start and end of each of the five chapters — with no replay, that is your only rewind button.
    • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the moment it is offered (see Positive Mental Attitude below) — it is the one thing the guides cannot promise you can go back for.
    • Turn cheats off for the whole run: using one disables trophies and saving.
    • Scan vehicles and study animals as you meet them; the counts (200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals) are checked on the 100% list, not on a single mission.

      What is still uncertain

      • Several hidden trophies have one-line official descriptions that name no place or step. Their guides give the best reading of the description and say plainly where the method is still being confirmed by the guide sites.
      • The one possibly missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, rests on PowerPyx and KeenGamer: the first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, may not be repeatable later, and it is not yet settled whether it counts toward the total.
      • Counts the game tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties, 9 activity types) come from the official list’s PSN progress targets as listed by PSNProfiles and the kit’s starter data.

      Your data stays yours

      Progress is saved only in this browser’s localStorage, under one key. Export downloads a JSON file with your earned trophies; import reads such a file back. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.
