Where the facts come from

Every trophy's official name, description and grade is shown as PlayStation publishes it. The list went public when the game unlocked in New Zealand on 18 November 2026, and the trackers agree on it: Exophase and PSNProfiles list the same 55 names, descriptions and grades. The counts in brackets (250 stunts, 42 shooting range challenges, 18 meditation spots and the rest) are the counts PlayStation tracks for each trophy.

  • PowerPyx GTA 6 trophy guide — the working guide behind most of the methods here: the three-stage roadmap, the Chapter 1 meditation warning, the counts and the unconfirmed methods. Sixshore's trophy and achievement list gathers its findings with Push Square's.
  • Push Square GTA 6 trophy guide — the second working guide: the 250+ hour estimate, the note that difficulty does not affect trophies, and the dissent that no trophy is known to be missable. It also marks 27 hidden trophies rather than 28, not counting Personal Best.
  • PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements — the trophy and achievement trackers that cross-check the list, the counts and the hidden flags (28 hidden: the 12 story trophies and 16 side trophies).
  • Planet GTA 6 trophy list — the full list of 55 with official names and descriptions, taken from Exophase and cross-checked with PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. planetgta6.com/en/gta-6-trophies/
  • One More Game trophy list — a second full listing of the 55, matching the others entry for entry. onemoregame.ph
  • Rockstar Games, GTA VI Trailer 2 — the setting this guide is written around: Leonida, Vice City, Lucia and Jason. No Rockstar artwork or logo is used anywhere in this atlas; the look is our own.

How this guide is honest about what it does not know

The game has only just come out, and several methods are still unconfirmed by the guide writers. Where a method is unconfirmed — the Ocean Beach cocktail, the showroom smash, the Payday Loans link, the UFO questline, the motorcycle kick — the guide says so in plain words instead of inventing a step. Check back as PowerPyx and Push Square pin those down.

Offline, by design

This atlas is static files only. It loads nothing over the network at run time: the trophy data lives in the app itself, your progress lives in your browser's local storage, and export and import are files on your own machine. The links above open only when you follow them.
